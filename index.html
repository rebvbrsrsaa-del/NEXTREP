ATHLETIC TRAINING • WEEK 1
Build your game, not just your gym numbers

Train to move
like an athlete.

A balanced starter plan for basketball strength, conditioning, coordination and recovery. The goal is to move well, get stronger over time and stay healthy.

3 daysstrength and movement
2 daysbasketball skills or easy conditioning
2 daysrest or gentle recovery

Your weekly plan

Keep reps smooth, never rushed.

Monday · Strength A

35–45 min

Full body basics

  • Warm-up walk or bike · 5–8 min
  • Bodyweight squat to a comfortable depth · 2×8
  • Incline push-ups · 2×8–12
  • Light dumbbell row · 2×10 each side
  • Glute bridge · 2×10
  • Dead bug · 2×6 each side

Tuesday · Skills

20–40 min

Basketball technique

  • Easy warm-up · 5 min
  • Stationary handles · 5 min
  • Form shooting close to the hoop · 10 min
  • Passing and footwork · 5–10 min
  • Finish with an easy cooldown

Wednesday · Recovery

Easy

Let your body adapt

  • Easy walk if comfortable
  • Gentle mobility, no forcing stretches
  • Drink water and get a full night of sleep

Thursday · Strength B

35–45 min

Control and stability

  • Warm-up · 5–8 min
  • Hip hinge with light dumbbells · 2×8
  • Wall or incline push-ups · 2×8–12
  • Resistance-band row or light row · 2×10
  • Calf raises, only if comfortable · 2×10
  • Bird dog · 2×6 each side

Friday · Skills + movement

20–35 min

Quality over intensity

  • Easy ball handling · 5 min
  • Spot shooting · 10–15 min
  • Gentle defensive slides only if pain-free and cleared
  • Cooldown walk

Saturday · Strength C

25–40 min

Light full-body practice

  • Warm-up · 5–8 min
  • Chair squat or sit-to-stand · 2×8
  • Light dumbbell press · 2×8–10
  • One-arm row · 2×10 each side
  • Glute bridge · 2×10
  • Side plank from knees · 2×15–20 sec each side

Sunday · Rest

Reset

No hard workout needed

  • Rest, relax and eat regular balanced meals
  • Notice what felt easy or difficult
  • Plan the next week around recovery

Quick training rules

  • 01
    Start easy
    Finish sets feeling like you could do a few more reps.
  • 02
    Build slowly
    Add reps or resistance gradually, not all at once.
  • 03
    Recover well
    Teens generally need 8–10 hours of sleep.

Fuel your training

No crash diets or skipping meals.

🥣 Before training

Have a familiar snack or meal with carbs and some protein, like yogurt and fruit or a sandwich.

🍽️ After training

Eat a balanced meal with protein, carbohydrates, fruit or vegetables, and enough food to feel satisfied.

💧 Hydration

Drink water throughout the day and keep water available during practice.

Important if you're returning from an injury
Only do movements your parent/guardian and physical therapist or clinician say are safe for you. If a movement causes pain, swelling, limping or your symptoms get worse, stop and tell a trusted adult. Skip jumping, sprinting and hard cutting until you're cleared for them.
